# Code of Conduct

Iterum is an open-source game engine and community project.

We want Iterum to be a place where people can work together, share ideas,
ask questions, and contribute without harassment or hostility.

## Expected Behavior

Members of the Iterum community are expected to:

* Treat others with respect.
* Give and accept constructive criticism.
* Discuss ideas and technical decisions in good faith.
* Focus criticism on the work, idea, or proposal rather than the person.
* Respect different levels of experience and knowledge.
* Keep discussions relevant to the project.
* Respect other people's privacy.
* Communicate clearly and avoid deliberately inflammatory or disruptive behavior.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in Iterum community spaces:

* Harassment, bullying, or intimidation.
* Discrimination or hateful conduct.
* Personal attacks, threats, or deliberate insults.
* Unwanted sexual attention or sexual harassment.
* Sharing someone's private or personal information without their permission.
* Deliberate disruption, spam, or malicious behavior.
* Repeatedly ignoring reasonable moderation requests.
* Other conduct that seriously disrupts participation or creates a hostile or unsafe environment.

## Project Discussions

Technical disagreements are a normal part of open-source development
and are welcome when they contribute to improving Iterum.

Disagreements should remain focused on the subject being discussed.
Criticism of a design, implementation, proposal, or decision is not the same as
criticism of the person who made it.

Disagreeing with a maintainer, contributor, or community member does not justify
personal attacks, harassment, or other unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within Iterum's official community
spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces
officially associated with the project.

It also applies when someone is publicly representing Iterum or acting in an
official project capacity.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Depending on the circumstances, severity, and history of an incident,
enforcement may include:

* Asking someone to stop or change their behavior.
* Removing inappropriate content.
* Closing, locking, or restricting issues, discussions, or pull requests.
* Temporarily restricting participation in project spaces.
* Permanently banning someone from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions will take the circumstances and severity of the incident
into account. Maintainers may consider the context, intent, impact, and whether
similar behavior has occurred previously.

Enforcement may be applied proportionally to the situation. Serious violations
may result in stronger action without prior warnings.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct,
please report it privately to the Iterum maintainers through the project's
designated contact method.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Information
from a report will only be shared when necessary to address the situation or
where required.

Reports should include enough information for the maintainers to understand
what happened, such as relevant links, dates, screenshots, or other context
where available.

## Good Faith

This Code of Conduct is intended to help maintain a respectful and productive
community, not to prevent disagreement, criticism, or honest mistakes.

People who make a mistake are encouraged to correct it and move forward.
Deliberate, repeated, or serious violations may result in stronger enforcement.
